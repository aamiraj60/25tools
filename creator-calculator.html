Why it matters

Why an estimate beats a guess when pricing your work

Brands price sponsorships against reach and engagement, not against how hard you worked. If you cannot quote a defensible rate, you anchor low and stay there. A calculator gives you a range grounded in the same inputs a media buyer uses: views per video, audience size, niche and geography. Walk into the negotiation with a number you can justify and you keep the value you created.

Ad revenue is equally misunderstood. RPM, the revenue per thousand views after YouTube's cut, varies wildly by topic. Finance and technology channels routinely earn several times what entertainment channels earn for the same view count, because advertisers pay more to reach that audience. Modelling RPM by niche stops you from extrapolating a viral hit into a permanent income forecast.

Engagement is the leading indicator that sits between reach and revenue. A channel with a million followers and a two percent engagement rate will often earn less than a channel with two hundred thousand followers and an eight percent rate, because the smaller audience actually acts. Tracking engagement health next to revenue shows you which lever to pull next.

FAQ

Creator earnings questions, answered

How accurate is the estimate?

It is a planning range, not a promise. Real earnings depend on watch time, ad rates, seasonality and your exact audience. The calculator applies transparent, published assumptions so you can see the reasoning and adjust the inputs to match your own analytics.

What is RPM and how is it different from CPM?

CPM is what an advertiser pays per thousand impressions. RPM is what you keep per thousand views after the platform's share and after accounting for views that showed no ad. RPM is the number that matters to a creator, and it varies widely by topic.

How much should I charge for a sponsorship?

A common starting point is a rate tied to your average views per video rather than your follower count. The calculator produces a range based on reach, niche and audience geography that you can use as an anchor in negotiation.

Which platforms are supported?

The model covers YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Twitch, and you can compare them side by side to see how the same audience performs on each.

Does it need access to my channel?

No. There is no OAuth login and no API connection. You enter approximate figures yourself, and nothing is transmitted to any server.

Is my data stored?

No. Every calculation happens in your browser and disappears when you close the tab. There is no account and no logging.

Why does niche change the estimate so much?

Advertisers pay different prices to reach different audiences. Finance, software and business topics attract high-value advertisers, while general entertainment attracts lower bids, so the same view count produces very different revenue.

Is the calculator free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no ads and no tracking.
