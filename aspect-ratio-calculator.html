Frequently asked questions

What is an aspect ratio?
Aspect ratio describes the proportional relationship between width and height. Common ratios: 16:9 (widescreen), 9:16 (vertical), 1:1 (square).
How does the dimension calculator work?
It uses the original ratio (W1:H1) and your new width (W2) to compute the new height: H2 = (H1 / W1) * W2. Or vice versa.
Can I use this for video editing and social media?
Absolutely. We've included presets for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, ultrawide, and more. The scale table helps you find 4K-ready dimensions.
