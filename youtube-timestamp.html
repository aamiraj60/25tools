Why it matters

Why timestamps turn a long video into a useful one

Long videos hide their best moments. A forty-minute talk might contain a two-minute explanation that answers a viewer's exact question, but without a timestamp they must scrub through the whole thing to find it. A jump-to link removes that friction and makes the value accessible in one click.

Chapters change how a video is navigated and indexed. A chapter list in the description lets viewers jump between sections and gives search engines a structured outline of the content, which can earn key-moment results in search. Generating correctly formatted chapter lines is the fiddly part, and it is exactly what this tool automates.

Embeds with a start time serve a different purpose. When you embed a video in a course, an article or a documentation page, starting it at the relevant moment keeps the reader's attention on the point you are making rather than making them wait for it to arrive.

FAQ

YouTube timestamp questions, answered

How do I create a YouTube timestamp link?

Paste the video URL and enter the time you want to link to. The tool returns a jump-to link that opens the video at that exact second, along with embed and chapter formats.

What time format does YouTube use in URLs?

YouTube uses a parameter that takes a whole number of seconds. The generator converts minutes or hours-minutes-seconds input into that value for you, so you never calculate it by hand.

How do I add chapters to a video description?

List each chapter as a timestamp followed by its title, with the first chapter starting at zero. The chapter builder produces lines in exactly that format, ready to paste into your description.

Why do my chapters not appear?

YouTube requires the first chapter to start at 0:00 and each chapter to be at least ten seconds long. If either rule is broken, the chapter list is ignored.

Can I link to a moment in a live stream?

Once a live stream has ended and been archived, its timestamps work like any other video. While it is still live, jumping to a past moment behaves differently.

Is my data uploaded?

No. The links are generated in your browser from the URL and time you enter. Nothing is transmitted, logged or stored.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no signup, no ads and no limit on how many timestamps you create.

Is the tool free?

Yes. It is completely free and carries no ads or tracking.
