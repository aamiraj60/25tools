Frequently asked questions

Is this YouTube transcript generator really free?

Yes — completely free, with no hidden limits, no account, and no credit card. The entire extraction and export pipeline runs locally in your browser.

Do I need to install a Chrome extension or any software?

Never. Unlike Tactiq, you don't install anything. Paste a link into the field above and the transcript appears — it works identically on desktop, tablet and mobile.

Can I download the transcript as an SRT or VTT subtitle file?

Absolutely. Use the export bar to download a properly formatted .SRT (with sequential indices and millisecond timing), a .VTT, a Markdown document, or a plain .TXT file.

Why does a video sometimes show no transcript?

If a creator disabled captions, made the video private, or the video is age-restricted, no transcript exists to extract. In that case you'll see the sample transcript instead — try another video or load the sample.

How does the AI summary work, and is my data private?

The summarizer runs an extractive scoring algorithm directly in your browser — it ranks sentences by keyword density and position. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or stored on any server.
